## Overall assessment

> *Weak backbone, incomplete empirical support for the prediction-set family, and the need to establish that the risk guarantees hold under the actual experimental protocol.*

All three concerns are addressed. Before running any new experiment we registered the revision protocol as Amendment 3 of the pre-registration (`docs/research/preregistration-mgcrc.md`).

- **Guarantee under the actual protocol.** We changed the protocol, not only the argument. All main results now use a frozen-model protocol, for which we prove the guarantee is exact (Proposition 2). Cross-fitting is analysed separately (Proposition 3) and confined to an appendix.
- **Backbone.** We replaced the backbone with two deep architectures: a U-Net with a ResNet-34 encoder and a U-Net with a Mix Transformer (MiT-B2) encoder.
- **Lesion equalization.** We re-test it under equal calibration data, with bootstrap CIs. It is described as unproven unless the pre-registered rule (H2) is met.

## 9. Limitations and conclusion

The limitations paragraph separates the limitations addressed in this revision from those that remain. **Addressed:** backbone, protocol and exchangeability, equal calibration data, small-lesion cut leakage, and statistics. **Remaining:** image-wise splits for 2-D datasets, small multi-lesion subgroups in Kvasir-SEG, marginal (not per-patient) guarantees, coarse FracAtlas annotations, and no clinical validation. The conclusion states the efficiency claim for lesion equalization only to the extent that H2 is met.
